Why invest in education

Nutrition is the
missing piece

Most clubs invest heavily in coaching, equipment and facilities. But athlete nutrition is often left to guesswork. One education session can change how your entire squad trains, recovers and performs.

Sessions are delivered by MSc. Kristýna Čočková — a qualified nutritionist and sport performance specialist who works with competitive athletes at all levels.

Better energy and focus in training

Athletes who fuel correctly train harder and absorb more from each session.

Faster recovery between sessions

Proper post-training nutrition reduces soreness and speeds up readiness for the next session.

Fewer injuries, less time off

Nutrition plays a direct role in injury risk — athletes who understand this miss fewer sessions through injury.

Peak performance on competition day

Competition-day fuelling is a skill. Athletes who practise it consistently perform at their best when it matters.
