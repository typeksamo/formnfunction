Techniques

Every treatment uses a combination of the techniques below — selected based on your specific condition, symptoms and goals.

01

ART

Active Release Technique — Certified Provider

ART is a patented, state-of-the-art soft tissue system that treats problems with muscles, tendons, ligaments, fascia and nerves. Non-invasive and precisely engineered to locate and quickly resolve soft-tissue disorders.

  • Overuse injuries and repetitive strain
  • Scar tissue and adhesions
  • Nerve entrapments and headaches
  • Back pain and postural issues
02

Dry Needling

Trigger Point Therapy

Fine needles are inserted into trigger points — hyperirritable spots in muscle tissue. When the trigger point releases, the client experiences immediate pain relief and improved range of motion.

  • Chronic muscle tightness and tension
  • Sports injuries and recovery
  • Referred pain patterns
  • Improved muscle activation
03

Sports Massage

Deep Tissue · Pregnancy · Relaxation

Sport and deep-tissue massage techniques to address muscular tension, enhance circulation and accelerate recovery — also offering pregnancy massage for expecting mothers.

  • Pre- and post-event sports massage
  • Deep tissue and myofascial release
  • Pregnancy massage
  • Relaxation and stress relief
Why it works

Recovery is part
of the training

Manual therapy isn't just for when you're injured. Proactive bodywork helps athletes and active people move better, avoid injury and perform at a consistently high level.

Faster recovery between sessions

Targeted treatment reduces DOMS, flushes metabolic waste and restores tissue quality for your next training.

Injury prevention and resilience

Identifying and treating tissue restrictions before they become injuries keeps you training consistently and progressing.

Improved range of motion and performance

Releasing restricted fascia and tight muscles directly improves movement quality — enabling better form, more strength and reduced compensation patterns.

Pain relief — acute and chronic

Whether it's a long-standing injury, postural pain or post-competition soreness — manual therapy targets the source, not just the symptom.
